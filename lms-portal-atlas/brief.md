# Brief: Atlas Learning, a course portal

The brief as the agent got it.

> Build the public portal for a learning platform on a fresh Raytha instance with the `raytha` CLI. No clicking
> around the admin.

## Look and feel

- Visually impressive, with a design of its own: its own palette and a Google Fonts pairing.
- Display type that is well proportioned, not overly wide.
- Responsive. It has to look good on a phone as well as a desktop.

## Content and pages

- Browse courses, course pages, and lessons grouped into modules.
- Some content gated behind a members-only area, using Raytha's login and member access.
- No forms that take payment.

## Done means

- `raytha check` passes with no broken routes.
- Screenshots of the result on desktop and mobile, including the signed-in view.
- A kit in this repository that rebuilds the site on a fresh install.
