# Brief: Lantern Ramblers, a Grateful Dead tribute band website

The brief as the agent got it.

> Build a website for a Grateful Dead cover band on a fresh Raytha instance with the `raytha` CLI. No clicking
> around the admin. Use a fictional band name, and no Grateful Dead logos or trademarks beyond describing it as a
> Grateful Dead tribute.

## Look and feel

- Psychedelic but tasteful, with a design of its own.
- Display type that is well proportioned, not overly wide or squished.
- Responsive. It has to look good on a phone as well as a desktop.

## Content and pages

- Tour dates, upcoming and past.
- A setlist archive with song stats.
- The band members, media and photos.
- Booking information with a way to get in touch. No payments.

## Done means

- `raytha check` passes with no broken routes.
- Screenshots of the result on desktop and mobile.
- A kit in this repository that rebuilds the site on a fresh install.
