# Brief: Larkmoor College, a college website

The brief as the agent got it.

> Build the public website of a fictional college on a fresh Raytha instance with the `raytha` CLI. No clicking around the admin.

## Look and feel

- Collegiate but modern. Readable serif headings, plain language, high contrast. No wide display fonts.
- Responsive. It has to look good on a phone as well as a desktop.
- Accessible markup: landmarks, a skip link, visible focus, alt text, labelled controls.
- Server-rendered templates. No client-side app in the layout.

## Content and pages

- A home page with a program finder, admissions, campus, events, news and athletics.
- Academics with programs you can filter by school and degree, and a page for each program.
- A faculty directory, admissions (how to apply, deadlines, cost and aid, visiting), campus life with a campus map,
  athletics with team schedules, news and events, about, contact and search.
- A members-only portal for students and faculty, with some resources limited to faculty and staff.
- No payment, application or registration forms. Every person and place is fictional.
- Make it obvious everywhere that the college and its people are fictional.

## Done means

- `raytha check` passes with no broken routes.
- Screenshots of the result on desktop and mobile, and a walkthrough video.
- A kit in the examples repository that rebuilds the site on a fresh install.
