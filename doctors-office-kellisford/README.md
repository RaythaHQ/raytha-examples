# Kellisford Family Medicine: a doctors office website

The website for Kellisford Family Medicine (fictional), an independent family-medicine practice in an invented town, built on Raytha. The home page is a day board of today's hours and who is in, followed by a visit-type chooser, a week board of providers, a four-step triage ladder with care guides, providers, sessions, notices and FAQ. Visit pages carry minutes, what to do before, what to bring and what happens after; and patients-only pages for prep sheets and after-visit notes are gated on the server. "Day board" design — no payment, registration, upload, booking, sign-up or contact forms. The kit suits an agency or freelancer building sites for independent clinics, family practices and specialty offices.

## Rebuild

```bash
RAYTHA_URL=https://your-site.example RAYTHA_API_KEY=... bash build.sh
```

7 content types (providers, visits, topics, updates, handouts, sessions, faq), 2 user groups (`patients`, `staff`), site pages (home, about, hours, new patients, when to call, week board, patient area, staff room, search), menus. Server-rendered Liquid only. `build.sh` generates the portraits and visit art if `seed/art` is missing, runs `../scripts/build-example.sh`, then runs the route check.

## How the pages work

- Each provider stores a pipe-separated `days` list (`mon|tue|wed|…`). Templates take today's weekday from `"now" | date: "%a" | downcase` and list who is in; the week board highlights today's column. Hours for the day come from a `case` on the weekday. No JavaScript.
- Visit types store minutes, a same-day flag, before/bring/after checklists. Care guides (`topics`) store a usual first step on the four-step ladder and signs at each step.
- Handouts and some sessions have an `audience` field (`public`, `patients`, `staff`). Templates read `CurrentUser.UserGroups` on the server.
- Search runs `get_content_items` with an OData `contains(...)` filter across seven content types, and hides staff/patient items from browsers that should not see them.

## Members-only areas

- Handouts and some sessions have an `audience` field (`public`, `patients`, `staff`).
- Templates read `CurrentUser.UserGroups` on the server and render either the content or a lock panel with a sign-in link. Staff see everything patients see; admins see everything.
- `/patients` (template `kf_patients`) shows a patient card, patient handouts and patient sessions, only for the `patients` group.
- `/staff` (template `kf_staff`) shows coverage notes, staff handouts and staff sessions, only for the `staff` group.
- To try it, create a public user in the admin, add them to `patients` and/or `staff`, and sign in at `/account/login`. There is no registration form on purpose.

## Notes

- Provider portraits and visit art (`seed/art/*.svg`) are generated by `seed/make-art.py`; `build.sh` runs it when the folder is missing. No real person or clinic is pictured.
- Dropdown values are rendered as their developer names and mapped to labels in the templates with `case`.
- Field values are captured into strings before they are compared.
- Everything is fictional: the practice, town, people, visits and figures. Phone numbers use the 555 range. Nothing on the site is medical advice.
