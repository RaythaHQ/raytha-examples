# Brief: Kellisford Family Medicine — a doctors office website (fictional)
Persona: Kellisford Family Medicine (fictional), an independent family-medicine practice in the invented town of Kellisford: seven people (physicians, NP, PA, nurses, front desk), same-day sick visits, annual physicals, labs, vaccines, video visits, care guides and a patients-only area. The site is built by an agency or freelancer for the clinic; the same build fits a practice with its own IT staff or a managed-services provider.
Design: "the day board" — chart-paper grid, warm paper, deep ink, teal for the practice and coral for same-day and emergency. Home shows today's weekday, hours and who is in, computed on the server from each provider's day list; a week board highlights today; a four-step triage ladder; visit types with what to bring. Schibsted Grotesk for headings, Figtree for text, DM Mono for hours and day codes. No wide display fonts.
IA: home (day board, visit chooser, week board, ladder teaser, notices, patient-area teaser); /visits; /week; /when-to-call (ladder + care guides); /care (topics); /providers; /sessions; /notices; /handouts; /questions; /hours; /new-patients; /about; /patients (patients-only); /staff (staff-only); /search.
Members: user groups `patients` and `staff`; gating is server-side in Liquid with CurrentUser.UserGroups. Gated bodies are never sent to the browser.
No payment, registration, upload, booking, sign-up or contact forms. Everything is labeled fictional; phone numbers use 555. Nothing on the site is medical advice.
Buyer target: self-hosted CMS for medical practices / open-source .NET CMS for doctors office websites; agencies and freelancers building for clinic clients.
Done: raytha check 0 failed, screenshots, walkthrough, rebuildable kit.
