# Brief: Postmark Trips, a surprise travel agency website

The brief as the agent got it.

> Build a website for a surprise travel agency on a fresh Raytha instance with the `raytha` CLI. No clicking around
> the admin. Use a fictional brand.

## Look and feel

- A distinct design of its own.
- Display type that is well proportioned, not overly wide or squished.
- Responsive. It has to look good on a phone as well as a desktop.

## Content and pages

- How it works.
- Trip tiers and price ranges, as information only. No checkout.
- Past reveals and destination stories.
- Reviews and an FAQ.
- A gift option, described. No payments.

## Done means

- `raytha check` passes with no broken routes.
- Screenshots of the result on desktop and mobile.
- A kit in this repository that rebuilds the site on a fresh install.
