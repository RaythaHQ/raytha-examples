# Brief: Saskell Creek Public Schools — a public school district website (fictional)
Persona: Saskell Creek Public Schools (fictional), a PreK–12 district in the invented town of Saskell Creek: sixteen schools on three feeder lines (Ridge, Creek, Mill), an early learning center and a career and technical center shared by all three. The communications office runs the site. The same build fits a district's own team, or an agency or freelancer building sites for school district clients.
Design: "feeder lines" — the district drawn as a transit map. Each high school zone is a colored line with its elementary, middle and high schools as stops, rendered on the server from the school records. Today's bells and status appear as a departure board; lunch appears as a week of trays; the calendar is a month grid with every day labeled in words. Paper, ink and bus-yellow, with line colors that never carry meaning alone. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for text and headings, Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono for times and codes. No wide display fonts.
IA: home; /schools (each school a stop: bells, status, programs, staff, dates), /calendar, /news, /lunch, /board (meetings with agendas and votes), /policies, /staff (directory by school), /resources (public, family and staff documents), /new-families, /families (members-only family hub), /staff-room (members-only), /about, /accessibility, /search.
Members: user groups `families` and `staff`; gating is server-side in Liquid with CurrentUser.UserGroups. Gated bodies are never sent to the browser.
No enrollment, registration, payment or contact forms. Everything is labeled fictional; phone numbers use 555.
Buyer target: self-hosted CMS for school districts / open-source .NET CMS for public schools.
Done: raytha check 0 failed, screenshots, walkthrough, rebuildable kit.
