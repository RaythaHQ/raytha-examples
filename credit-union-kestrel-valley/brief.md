# Brief: Kestrel Valley Credit Union, a credit union website

The brief as the agent got it.

> Build the public website of a fictional credit union on a fresh Raytha instance with the `raytha` CLI. No clicking around the admin.

## Look and feel

- Calm, trustworthy and local. Plain language, high contrast, big tap targets. No wide display fonts.
- Responsive. It has to look good on a phone as well as a desktop.
- Accessible markup: landmarks, a skip link, visible focus, alt text, labelled controls, real tables for rates.

## Content and pages

- A home page with today's rates, top tasks, featured products, branches, learning, security and news.
- Rates with an effective date, accounts and loans, branches and ATMs, financial education, a security center with fraud alerts, the board, reports and documents, an accessibility statement, disclosures and contact.
- A members-only area for annual meeting materials and board elections.
- No application, payment or registration forms, and no fake online banking login. Opening an account points to a branch or a phone number, and online banking links out.
- Make it obvious everywhere that the institution, its rates and its people are fictional.

## Done means

- `raytha check` passes with no broken routes.
- Screenshots of the result on desktop and mobile, and a walkthrough video.
- A kit in the examples repository that rebuilds the site on a fresh install.
