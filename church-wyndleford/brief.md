# Brief: Wyndleford Parish Church — a church website (fictional)
Persona: Wyndleford Parish Church (fictional), a stone church on the green in the fictional town of Wyndleford since 1887, following the church year and the Revised Common Lectionary. A rector, an associate rector and a deacon, a director of music, a children and families lead, a parish administrator and a senior warden. Three services every Sunday, eight ministries including a food pantry, nine small groups, sixteen member households in the directory.
Design: "bulletin and ribbon" — a classic centered masthead with an arched-window emblem, a season ribbon across the top of every page, warm paper and stone tones, arched cards for ministries, and an accent color that follows the liturgical season. Headings in EB Garamond, interface text in Public Sans, dates, times and hymn numbers in DM Mono. No wide display fonts.
IA: home (this Sunday's bulletin, the next seven days, ministries, recent sermons, members band, clergy, plan a visit); /year (the church year wheel and seasons, ?s= preview) and season pages; /sundays and Sunday pages (readings, hymns, sermon); /calendar (month grid by ?m=) and event pages; /ministries; /groups; /people; /visit; /members, /directory, /prayer, /rota (members); /search.
Server-side logic: current season from today's date and the season dates; ribbon widths and wheel arcs from day counts; next Sunday; seven-day strip and month grid from timestamps; member's own rota slots; prayer requests filtered by audience.
Members: user groups `members` and `clergy`; gating is server-side in Liquid with CurrentUser.UserGroups. Gated content is never sent to the browser.
No giving, payment, registration or sign-up forms. Everything is labeled fictional.
Buyer target: self-hosted CMS for churches / open-source church website CMS on .NET; churches, dioceses and the agencies and freelancers that build their sites.
