# Brief: Maren & Ezra, a wedding website for guests

The brief as the agent got it.

> Build a wedding website for guests on a fresh Raytha instance with the `raytha` CLI. No clicking around the
> admin.

## Look and feel

- Visually impressive, with a design of its own.
- Display type that is well proportioned, not overly wide or squished.
- Responsive. It has to look good on a phone as well as a desktop.

## Content and pages

- The couple's story, the schedule of events, travel and hotel blocks, things to do, an FAQ, registry links to
  other sites, and a photo gallery.
- A working RSVP that saves replies privately, with spam protection. No payment forms.
- Optionally, a page only guests can see after signing in.

## Done means

- `raytha check` passes with no broken routes.
- Screenshots of the result on desktop and mobile.
- A kit in this repository that rebuilds the site on a fresh install.
