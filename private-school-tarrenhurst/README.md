# Tarrenhurst School: a private school website

The website for Tarrenhurst School (fictional), an independent Pre-K to Grade 12 day school in an invented town, built on Raytha. The home page reads like a printed prospectus: a cover with today's six-day rotation day and Upper School blocks worked out on the server, then seven numbered chapters covering the three divisions and "a day in the life" of each, the course of studies with signature courses, the House Cup standings, athletics and arts by season, admissions with dated steps and tuition, and the term calendar and stories. A families portal and a faculty common room are gated on the server. "Prospectus" design, with no payment, application, registration, inquiry or sign-up forms. The kit suits an agency or freelancer building sites for private, independent, day and boarding schools.

## Rebuild

```bash
RAYTHA_URL=https://your-site.example RAYTHA_API_KEY=... bash build.sh
```

8 content types (divisions, courses, faculty, houses, programs, events, stories, letters), 2 user groups (`families`, `faculty`), site pages (home, admissions, about, visit, the rotation, families portal, faculty common room, search), menus. Server-rendered Liquid only. `build.sh` generates the division plates, house banners, story plates and faculty monograms if `seed/art` is missing, then runs `../scripts/build-example.sh`.

## How the pages work

- **Six-day rotation.** Templates take the day of the year from `"now" | date: "%j"`, scale it to school days (times 5, divided by 7) and take the result modulo 6 to get Day A to Day F; on weekends the pages say there are no classes and show where the rotation stands. Seven blocks (A–G) rotate through five periods, so the cover, the rotation wheel (inline SVG) and the grid on `/rotation` all show today's blocks and the courses that meet in them. No JavaScript.
- **Divisions** store grades, enrollment, class size, hallmarks and a `day` field (`time~what|time~what|…`) that renders as the "day in the life" ledger.
- **Courses** store department, division, level (including `signature`), grades, rotation block, credits, prerequisites and units. `/courses` filters by department and division from query-string parameters with an OData filter on the server.
- **Houses** store points, a color and a ledger (`event~points|…`). The House Cup sorts houses by points and draws bars as a percentage of the leader.
- **Events** store term, kind, audience, day and month for the calendar date tiles; **programs** store kind and season for the athletics and arts columns.
- Search runs `get_content_items` with an OData `contains(...)` filter across eight content types, and hides the contents of families- and faculty-only items from browsers that should not see them.

## Members-only areas

- Letters and events have an `audience` field (`public`, `families`, `faculty`).
- Templates read `CurrentUser.UserGroups` on the server and render either the content or a lock panel with a sign-in link. Faculty see everything families see; admins see everything. Lists show members-only titles with a lock and leave their contents out; the page description in the HTML head uses a generic line for members-only items.
- `/families` (template `th_families`) shows a family card, family dates, this week's notices and letters home, only for the `families` group.
- `/common-room` (template `th_common`) shows duties, faculty dates, rotas, minutes and grading deadlines, only for the `faculty` group.
- To try it, create a public user in the admin, add them to `families` and/or `faculty`, and sign in at `/account/login`. There is no registration form on purpose.

## Notes

- Division plates, house banners, story plates and faculty monograms (`seed/art/*.svg`) are generated by `seed/make-art.py`; `build.sh` runs it when the folder is missing. No real person or school is pictured.
- Dropdown values are rendered as their developer names and mapped to labels in the templates with `case`.
- Field values are captured into strings before they are compared; numbers are converted with `plus: 0`.
- Web template includes are not used; shared pieces are inlined in each template.
- Everything is fictional: the school, town, people, houses, courses and figures. Phone numbers use the 555 range.
