# Brief: Tarrenhurst School — a private school website (fictional)
Persona: Tarrenhurst School (fictional), an independent Pre-K to Grade 12 day school of about 620 students in the invented town of Tarrenhurst: Lower, Middle and Upper School, eight academic departments, four houses with a House Cup, athletics and arts by season, a six-day rotation schedule, admissions with tuition and aid, a families portal and a faculty common room. The site is built by an agency or freelancer for the school; the same build fits a school with its own IT or communications staff.
Design: "the prospectus" — ivory paper, navy ink, oxblood for the school and gilt for chapter numerals, a letterhead with a crest, thin double rules and drop caps. The home page is a cover plus chapters I–VII with a sticky chapter rail. The cover shows today's rotation day (A–F) and Upper School blocks, computed on the server from the date, drawn as an SVG wheel. Fraunces for headings, Hanken Grotesk for text, Spline Sans Mono for codes, dates and labels. No wide display fonts. Nothing like a public school district site.
IA: home (cover and rotation, divisions and day-in-the-life ledger, course of studies with signature courses, House Cup, athletics and arts, admissions steps and tuition line, calendar and stories, portal band); /divisions; /courses (department and division filters); /faculty; /houses; /programs; /calendar; /stories; /letters; /rotation; /admissions; /about; /visit; /families (families only); /common-room (faculty only); /search.
Members: user groups `families` and `faculty`; gating is server-side in Liquid with CurrentUser.UserGroups. Gated bodies are never sent to the browser.
No payment, application, registration, inquiry or sign-up forms. Everything is labeled fictional; phone numbers use 555.
Buyer target: self-hosted CMS for private schools / open-source .NET CMS for independent schools; agencies and freelancers building for private school clients.
Done: raytha check 0 failed, screenshots, walkthrough, rebuildable kit.
