# Brief: City of Juniper Falls, a local city government website

The brief as the agent got it.

> Build the website of a fictional city government on a fresh Raytha instance with the `raytha` CLI. No clicking around the admin.

## Look and feel

- Clear, calm and trustworthy. Built for residents first: big tap targets, plain language, high contrast. No wide display fonts.
- Responsive. It has to look good on a phone as well as a desktop.
- Accessible markup: landmarks, a skip link, visible focus, alt text, labelled form controls.

## Content and pages

- A home page with an alert banner, search, top tasks, meetings, public notices, news and events.
- "How do I..." service pages (pay, apply, report, request, find), city departments with contact details, the mayor and council.
- Public meetings with agendas, minutes and video, boards and commissions, public notices (hearings, bids, ordinances), news, a community calendar.
- Trash and recycling schedule, an accessibility statement, and a contact page.
- Feeds residents can subscribe to: a calendar of public meetings and an RSS feed of notices.
- No payment or registration forms. Paying a bill or reporting an issue links out to the city's existing systems.

## Done means

- `raytha check` passes with no broken routes.
- Screenshots of the result on desktop and mobile, and a walkthrough video.
- A kit in this repository that rebuilds the site on a fresh install.
