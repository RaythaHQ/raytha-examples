# Brief: Low Tide, a music festival website

The brief as the agent got it.

> Build a website for a music festival on a fresh Raytha instance with the `raytha` CLI. No clicking around the
> admin.

## Look and feel

- Visually impressive, with a design of its own. No wide display fonts.
- Responsive. It has to look good on a phone as well as a desktop.

## Content and pages

- A lineup with filters, a schedule by stage and day, and a page for every artist.
- A venue map and venue information, and an FAQ.
- No ticket purchasing. The ticket button goes to a placeholder external link.

## Done means

- `raytha check` passes with no broken routes.
- Screenshots of the result on desktop and mobile, and a walkthrough video.
- A kit in this repository that rebuilds the site on a fresh install.
