# Brief: Halflight — a podcast and magazine website (fictional)
Persona: Halflight (fictional), an independent publisher in the fictional city of Corran with a weekly podcast and a quarterly print magazine about people who work the night shift. Host Mara Quint, editor Tobiah Rendle, five more contributors. Season two of the podcast (eight episodes, a trailer and two members-only bonus episodes) runs alongside Issue 9 (Autumn 2026) and Issue 10 (Winter 2026, at the printer). Each episode follows one night worker: a baker, a track gang lead, a night nurse, a fish market porter, an overnight radio host, a snowplow driver, a hotel night auditor, a bridge tender, and next a 911 dispatcher.
Design: "night clock and contents page" — a dark navy 24-hour dial and player, warm paper for reading, a lamp-amber accent, magazine covers with a clock, contents pages with large page numbers. Headings and long reading in Newsreader, interface text in IBM Plex Sans, times and labels in JetBrains Mono. No wide display fonts. Nothing like a newsroom front page with sections and breaking banners, or a band tour page.
IA: home (latest episode, night clock, on shift now, next episode, current issue contents, running order, night mail, back room); /listen and episode pages (audio, waveform, chapters, show notes, transcript, shift bar, issue, letters); /issues and issue pages (cover, editor's letter, contents, episodes); /read and story pages (section filter); /hours (hour index by query parameter); /mail and letter pages; /people and contributor pages; /about; /members (members); /desk and /rundown (editors); /search.
Server-side logic: shift arcs and the clock hand from stored hours and the current time; who is on shift now, including overnight shifts; future episodes shown as coming with notes held back; contents pages built from stories by issue and page; the hour index; editors' due, late and days-to-air counts.
Members: user groups `members` and `editors`; gating is server-side in Liquid with CurrentUser.UserGroups. Gated content is never sent to the browser.
No payment, subscription, registration or sign-up forms. Everything is labeled fictional.
Buyer target: self-hosted CMS for podcasts and magazines / open-source .NET CMS for a digital magazine; independent publishers, podcast networks and the agencies that build for them.
Done: raytha check 0 failed, screenshots, walkthrough, rebuildable kit.
