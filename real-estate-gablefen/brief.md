# Brief: Gablefen Realty — a real estate website (fictional)
Persona: Gablefen Realty (fictional), an independent residential brokerage opened in 1978 in the mill town of Gablefen, with a bay-window office at 12 Courthouse Square. Rowan Ellison is the broker and owner (second generation); four other agents work the five parts of town: Mill Reach, West Common, Ridge Lane, Ferry Green and Courthouse Square. Sixteen homes are on the books (for sale, coming soon, under contract or sold), with weekly open houses, a plat the office still keeps in a book, and two desks behind sign-in: one for buyer clients and one for agents. The site is built for the brokerage by an agency or freelancer, or by the firm itself; the same build fits a small independent office that wants to own its public site.
Design: "the listing window" — a high-street estate agent's bay window: cream paper, brick and brass, a dark window frame, hanging property cards. Headings in Figtree, body text in Newsreader, prices, file numbers and labels in IBM Plex Mono. No wide display fonts. Nothing like a city park kiosk, a charity station board, or a wedding weekend schedule.
IA: home (listing window of hanging cards with elevations drawn from style, open-this-weekend board, plat teaser, recently sold); /listings (filter by status and neighborhood); listing detail (elevation, facts, floor plan from rooms, days on market; private remarks for agents only); /plat (neighborhood washes and numbered pins from content); /open-houses (happening now, today, this weekend); /sold; /neighborhoods and neighborhood detail; /styles and style detail; /agents; /buy; /sell; /about; /clients (buyer clients only); /desk (agents only); /search.
Server-side logic: open-house status (happening now / today / this weekend) from stored weekday and minutes; house elevations from style, stories, siding and roof; floor plans from a rooms field; days on the market from the listed date; plat pins and neighborhood washes from stored map data.
Members: user groups `clients` and `agents`; gating is server-side in Liquid with CurrentUser.UserGroups. Gated bodies and private remarks are never sent to the browser.
No offer, payment, registration or sign-up forms. Offers are written at the office. Everything is labeled fictional; phone numbers use 555.
Buyer target: self-hosted CMS for real estate agencies / open-source .NET CMS for realtor websites; brokerages and the agencies and freelancers who build for them.
