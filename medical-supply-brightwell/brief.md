# Brief: Brightwell Medical Supply, a DME provider website

The brief as the agent got it.

> Build a corporate website for a durable medical equipment provider on a fresh Raytha instance with the `raytha`
> CLI. No clicking around the admin.

## Look and feel

- Visually impressive, with a design of its own: its own palette and a Google Fonts pairing.
- Display type that is well proportioned, not overly wide.
- Responsive. It has to look good on a phone as well as a desktop.

## Content and pages

- Product categories and products, with the details patients and caregivers look for.
- Patient resources, insurance and how-to-order information, FAQs and locations.
- No payment forms.

## Done means

- `raytha check` passes with no broken routes.
- Screenshots of the result on desktop and mobile.
- A kit in this repository that rebuilds the site on a fresh install.
