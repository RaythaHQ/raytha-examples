# Brief: Harlow Creek Health System, a hospital / health system website

The brief as the agent got it.

> Build the public website of a fictional hospital / health system on a fresh Raytha instance with the `raytha` CLI. No clicking around the admin.

## Look and feel

- Calm, trustworthy healthcare design. Plain language, high contrast, big tap targets. No wide display fonts.
- Responsive. It has to look good on a phone as well as a desktop.
- Accessible markup: landmarks, a skip link, visible focus, alt text, labelled controls.

## Content and pages

- A home page with find-a-doctor, locations, featured services, health classes, news and patient shortcuts.
- Locations and facilities, departments and services, a providers directory with specialty and location filters, patient and visitor information, health news, careers, events and classes.
- A staff-only portal for clinician resources (policies, onboarding, formulary notes).
- No payment or registration forms. No real patient data; every person is fictional.
- Make it obvious everywhere that the institution and its people are fictional.

## Done means

- `raytha check` passes with no broken routes.
- Screenshots of the result on desktop and mobile, and a walkthrough video.
- A kit in the examples repository that rebuilds the site on a fresh install.
