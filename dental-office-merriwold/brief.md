# Brief: Merriwold Dental — a dental office website (fictional)
Persona: Merriwold Dental (fictional), an independent family dental practice at 22 Ferry Street in the fictional town of Tansey Ford, opened in 2014 by Dr. Imogen Ashdown. Three dentists (general, implants and oral surgery, orthodontics), two hygienists and a treatment coordinator; four chairs, open six days a week. Fifteen treatments from checkups to implants, eighteen guides and forms, thirteen front-desk answers and six chairside notes.
Design: "left rail and tooth map" — a dark spruce left rail with the practice name, navigation, phone and hours instead of a top header; porcelain and paper tones, a soft gum pink behind the chart, mint for highlighted teeth and coral for the selected one. Headings in Source Serif 4, interface text in Figtree, tooth numbers, sheet codes and minutes in IBM Plex Mono. No wide display fonts. Nothing like the Kellisford doctors office day board or triage ladder.
IA: home (tooth map, treatment ledger, chairside notes, team, patient room, answers); /chart (tooth map by ?t= tooth and ?f= family); /treatments and treatment pages (stats, visit by visit, how it feels, where in the mouth, who does it, aftercare sheet); /library and guide pages (recovery timeline); /answers and answer pages; /team and team member pages; /notes and note pages; /visit (first visit minute by minute, hours, address, insurance); /patients (patients); /desk and /huddle (team); /search.
Server-side logic: tooth highlights from stored tooth numbers; treatments, answers and notes per tooth; family highlights; visit and chair-time totals; recovery timelines; open-now chip and today's hours row from the clock; huddle counts for today, carried over, coming up and done.
Members: user groups `patients` and `team`; gating is server-side in Liquid with CurrentUser.UserGroups. Gated content is never sent to the browser.
No booking, payment, registration or sign-up forms, and no patient records. Printable forms are fictional samples. Everything is labeled fictional.
Buyer target: self-hosted CMS for dental practice websites / open-source .NET CMS for dental offices; dental practices, dental groups and the agencies and freelancers that build their sites.
Done: raytha check 0 failed, screenshots, walkthrough, rebuildable kit.
