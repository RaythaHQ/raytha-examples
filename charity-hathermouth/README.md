# Hathermouth River Rescue: a charity website

The website for Hathermouth River Rescue (fictional), an independent volunteer river rescue charity on an invented river, built on Raytha. The home page is built like a station board: this week's crew watch and today's tides worked out on the server, a flip-board count of this year's callouts, a schematic river map with every callout plotted by river mile, the pager log with callouts counted by kind, the open appeal with its progress and what gifts pay for, the fleet, six safety rules, volunteer roles and events. Ways to give are described without a form, with where each dollar went. A crew room and a trustees' area are gated on the server. "Station board" design, with no donation, payment, application, registration or sign-up forms. The kit suits an agency or freelancer building sites for charities and nonprofits.

## Rebuild

```bash
RAYTHA_URL=https://your-site.example RAYTHA_API_KEY=... bash build.sh
```

8 content types (watches, callouts, fleet, roles, appeals, advice, events, notices), 2 user groups (`crew`, `trustees`), site pages (home, the river, ways to give, about, crew room, trustees, search), menus. Server-rendered Liquid only. `build.sh` generates the boat plates if `seed/art` is missing, then runs `../scripts/build-example.sh`.

## How the pages work

- **The watch on call.** Templates take the day of the year and the day of the week from `"now" | date`, work out the week number, and take it modulo 4 to pick one of the four watches (sorted by `sort_order`). The station board, the watches page (with an eight-week rota) and the crew card all use it. No JavaScript.
- **Tides (illustrative).** High water starts from a fixed minute and moves on 50 minutes a day through a 12 h 25 min cycle, so the board lists today's high and low waters and draws a tide curve: an inline SVG path generated once, shifted by the day's first high water, with a "now" line. The times are for a fictional river.
- **The river map.** An inline SVG schematic of the river (weir, mill pond, town bridge, the station, marina, saltmarsh, the Bar). Each callout stores a `mile` (0 to 12); the template places a dot at `60 + mile × 90` and colors it by kind, and each dot links to its report. The callout detail page reuses the map with a single ringed dot.
- **Callouts** store reference, kind, reach, mile, date, time paged, time on service, boat, watch, people helped and outcome. `/callouts` filters by kind and reach from query-string parameters on the server; the bar chart counts callouts by kind and scales to the largest.
- **Appeals** store a target, the amount raised, donors and a `buys` field (`amount~item|…`). Progress bars are drawn from the two numbers on the server; the gift ladder is split from `buys`.
- **Ways to give** is a site page with widgets: eight ways to give (no form), where each dollar went as a stacked bar, and donor questions.
- Search runs `get_content_items` with an OData `contains(...)` filter across eight content types, and hides the contents of crew- and trustee-only items from browsers that should not see them.

## Members-only areas

- Notices and events have an `audience` field (`public`, `crew`, `trustees`).
- Templates read `CurrentUser.UserGroups` on the server and render either the content or a lock panel with a sign-in link. Admins see everything. Lists show members-only titles with a lock and leave their contents out; the page description in the HTML head uses a generic line for members-only items.
- `/crew-room` (template `hr_crew`) shows a crew card with this week's watch, today's tides, training nights, this week at the station and every procedure, kit check and bulletin, only for the `crew` group.
- `/trustees` (template `hr_trustees`) shows meetings, an appeal tracker, the decisions due and the minutes, board papers, risk register and management accounts, only for the `trustees` group. Each area points a signed-in member of the other group back to their own.
- To try it, create a public user in the admin, add them to `crew` and/or `trustees`, and sign in at `/account/login`. There is no registration form on purpose.

## Notes

- Boat plates (`seed/art/*.svg`) are generated by `seed/make-art.py`; `build.sh` runs it when the folder is missing. No real boat, person or place is pictured.
- Dropdown values are rendered as their developer names and mapped to labels in the templates with `case`.
- Number fields are converted with `append: "" | plus: 0` before arithmetic; field values are captured into strings before they are compared.
- Web template includes are not used; shared pieces are inlined in each template.
- Everything is fictional: the charity, river, town, people, boats, callouts and figures. Phone numbers use the 555 range. Safety advice is written for a fictional river.
