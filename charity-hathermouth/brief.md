# Brief: Hathermouth River Rescue — a charity website (fictional)
Persona: Hathermouth River Rescue (fictional), an independent volunteer rescue charity founded in 1974 that covers twelve miles of the invented River Hather, from Hather Weir to the Bar where it meets the sea: two inshore lifeboats, a flood rescue boat and a response vehicle, four crew watches that take the pager a week at a time, about 60 volunteers, six trustees, and no government running costs. The site is built by an agency or freelancer for the charity; the same build fits a charity with its own IT or communications volunteer.
Design: "the station board" — chart-paper backgrounds with a grid, deep navy, signal orange for anything urgent, sea-glass teal for water, hazard-stripe safety cards. The home page opens on a station board (this week's watch, today's illustrative tides with a tide curve, a flip-board count of callouts), then a schematic river map with this year's callouts plotted by river mile. Archivo for headings, Source Serif 4 for text, IBM Plex Mono for references, times and labels. No wide display fonts. Nothing like a grantmaking foundation or a membership association site.
IA: home (station board, river map, pager log and callouts by kind, open appeal with a gift ladder, fleet, six safety rules, volunteer roles, events); /river (map, reach-by-reach table, tides); /callouts (kind and reach filters); /fleet; /watches (on-call watch and an eight-week rota); /volunteer; /appeals; /give (eight ways to give, where each dollar went, donor questions); /safety; /events; /notices; /about (figures, history, trustees); /crew-room (crew only); /trustees (trustees only); /search.
Members: user groups `crew` and `trustees`; gating is server-side in Liquid with CurrentUser.UserGroups. Gated bodies are never sent to the browser.
No donation, payment, volunteer application, registration or sign-up forms. Ways to give are described (check, bank transfer, standing order, payroll giving, employer matching, gift in a will, fundraising, the shop). Everything is labeled fictional; phone numbers use 555; tide times are illustrative.
Buyer target: self-hosted CMS for charities / open-source .NET CMS for nonprofits; agencies and freelancers building for charity clients.
