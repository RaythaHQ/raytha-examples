# Brief: Cadwallow Park — a city park website (fictional)
Persona: Cadwallow Park (fictional), a 410-acre city park opened in 1889 and run by the city's Parks and Recreation Department: a 14-acre lake called the Wallow, a wooded ridge up to Hillcrest (742 ft), a 41-acre long meadow, four gates, five blazed trails (10.6 miles), a 1928 boathouse, a 1911 walled garden, shelters and ballfields, a register of notable trees, and the Friends of Cadwallow Park, a volunteer group that runs workdays and raises money. The site is built for the parks department by an agency or the city's IT team; the same build fits a county parks department or a park conservancy.
Design: "the trailhead kiosk" — park wayfinding: routed timber signs in brown with cream lettering, deep forest green, a kraft-paper background, blaze colors on white trail markers. The home page opens on a kiosk map board (an SVG park map with trail lines and numbered places from content, "you are here" at the South Gate) beside a "today at the park" panel worked out on the server. Overpass for headings, Literata for text, Overpass Mono for labels. No wide display fonts. Nothing like a city hall site (services, council meetings, notices).
IA: home (kiosk map, today at the park, open-now board, look for this month, trails with profiles, conditions board, park projects, events, Friends); /map (layers: trails and places, tree register; places by area); /places (kind and open-now filters); /trails (blazes, profiles); /trees (tag cards and map); /look-for (month filter and a year chart); /conditions; /projects (phase tracker, budget); /events; /bulletins; /visit (figures, getting here, rules, questions); /shelters-and-fields (capacity, season, status, how to reserve by phone, fees); /about; /stewards (friends only); /crew (staff only); /search.
Server-side logic: gates open/closed from the time; approximate sunrise/sunset from a monthly table interpolated by day; every place's open/closing soon/opens later/out of season status from stored hours and season months (including seasons that wrap the new year and closing at dusk); this month's sightings from stored months; elevation profiles from stored elevations; trunk cross-sections from girth; phase steps and funding bars from stored numbers.
Members: user groups `friends` and `staff`; gating is server-side in Liquid with CurrentUser.UserGroups. Gated bodies are never sent to the browser.
No booking, payment, registration, volunteer or sign-up forms. Shelters and fields are reserved by phone through the parks office. Everything is labeled fictional; phone numbers use 555; sunrise and sunset are approximate.
Buyer target: self-hosted CMS for city parks departments / open-source .NET CMS for parks and recreation; city IT teams and agencies building for parks departments.
Done: raytha check 0 failed, screenshots, walkthrough, rebuildable kit.
