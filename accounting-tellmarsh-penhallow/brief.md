# Brief: Tellmarsh & Penhallow CPAs — an accounting firm website (fictional)
Persona: Tellmarsh & Penhallow CPAs (fictional), a two-partner CPA firm with about twenty-six people in the invented river town of Merrow Bend: individual and business tax returns, monthly bookkeeping, payroll, nonprofit 990s, notice response, estates and year-round planning. The site is built by an agency or freelancer for the firm; the same build fits a firm with its own IT staff.
Design: "the tax-year dial" — warm paper, deep ink and one color per quarter (teal, green, ochre, rust). The home page hero is a twelve-month dial with every deadline placed by day of year, a hand at today and a countdown to the next deadline; calendar tiles for upcoming dates; a ruled memo pad for side panels. Newsreader for headings, Instrument Sans for text, IBM Plex Mono for dates, forms and fees. No wide display fonts.
IA: home (dial, next five dates, services with fees, the year by quarter, who we serve, records chart, people, field notes, client portal teaser); /deadlines (month-by-month almanac, server-side filter by who it applies to; deadline pages with form, late and extension rules, a dial and the covering service); /services (fixed fees, included, what to bring, lead, dates covered); /who-we-serve; /people; /notes; /keep (records-retention chart); /documents (public, clients, staff); /fees; /new-clients (no form); /about; /client-portal (members-only, clients); /staff-room (members-only, staff); /search.
Members: user groups `clients` and `staff`; gating is server-side in Liquid with CurrentUser.UserGroups. Gated bodies are never sent to the browser.
No payment, registration, upload, sign-up or contact forms. Everything is labeled fictional; phone numbers use 555. Dates are typical federal dates, for orientation.
Buyer target: self-hosted CMS for accounting firms / open-source .NET CMS for CPA firm websites; agencies and freelancers building for accounting-firm clients.
Done: raytha check 0 failed, screenshots, walkthrough, rebuildable kit.
