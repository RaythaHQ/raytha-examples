# Tellmarsh & Penhallow CPAs: an accounting firm website

The website for Tellmarsh & Penhallow CPAs (fictional), a two-partner CPA firm in an invented river town, built on Raytha. The home page is a tax-year dial with every deadline placed by day of year and a countdown to the next one, followed by the next five dates, fixed-fee services, the year by quarter, who the firm serves, a records-retention chart, people and field notes. A month-by-month deadline almanac, service pages with what is included and what to bring, and members-only pages for clients and staff gated on the server. "Tax-year dial" design — no payment, registration, upload, sign-up or contact forms. The kit suits an agency or freelancer building sites for accounting firms, bookkeepers and enrolled agents.

## Rebuild

```bash
RAYTHA_URL=https://your-site.example RAYTHA_API_KEY=... bash build.sh
```

7 content types (people, services, deadlines, industries, notes, records, documents), 2 user groups (`clients`, `staff`), site pages (home, about, fees, new clients, client portal, staff room, search), menus. Server-rendered Liquid only. `build.sh` generates the portraits, industry art and field-note art if `seed/art` is missing, runs `../scripts/build-example.sh`, then runs the route check.

## How the pages work

- Each deadline stores its `month`, `day`, day of year (`doy`) and a dial `lane`. Templates work out today's day of year on the server from `"now" | date: "%m"` and `"%d"` and a month-start table, so the countdowns, the struck-through past dates and the current month stay right without edits.
- The dial ring is a static SVG. Each pin is a link positioned with `transform: rotate(<doy × 360 / 365>deg) translateY(-<lane radius>cqw)`, computed in Liquid; the today hand uses the same rotation. No JavaScript.
- "Days until" wraps into next year: `doy - today`, plus 365 when negative. The next deadline is the smallest value; the next five are the deadlines from today onward, then from January.
- `/deadlines?for=business` (or individuals, payroll, nonprofit, everyone) filters on the server.
- Services link to deadlines (`service` dropdown on each deadline) and people (`lead`); notes link to their `author`. Detail pages pull the related items with `get_content_items` and an OData filter.
- Records store `years` (99 = keep permanently); `/keep` draws each as a bar, striped when permanent.
- Search runs `get_content_items` with an OData `contains(...)` filter across seven content types.

## Members-only areas

- Documents have an `audience` field (`public`, `clients`, `staff`).
- Templates read `CurrentUser.UserGroups` on the server and render either the document or a lock panel with a sign-in link. Staff see everything clients see; admins see everything. Staff procedures are left out of the documents list and search for everyone else.
- `/client-portal` (template `tp_portal`) shows a client file card, the days to the next date, the engagement stage for the current quarter, client checklists and how-tos, and the notices and engagement table from the page's widgets, only for the `clients` group.
- `/staff-room` (template `tp_staff`) shows deadlines in the next 45 days, the season board, staff notices and procedures, only for the `staff` group.
- To try it, create a public user in the admin, add them to `clients` and/or `staff`, and sign in at `/account/login`. There is no registration form on purpose.

## Notes

- Portraits are generated monograms and the industry and field-note art (`seed/art/*.svg`) is generated by `seed/make-art.py`; `build.sh` runs it when the folder is missing. No real person or business is pictured.
- Dropdown values are rendered as their developer names and mapped to labels in the templates with `case`.
- Field values are captured into strings before they are compared, and number fields are captured before arithmetic.
- Everything is fictional: the firm, town, people, clients, fees and figures. Phone numbers use the 555 range. Deadlines are typical federal dates for calendar-year filers, shown for orientation.
