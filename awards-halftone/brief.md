# Brief: Halftone Awards, an awards program website

The brief as the agent got it.

> Build a website for an awards program on a fresh Raytha instance with the `raytha` CLI. No clicking around the
> admin.

## Look and feel

- Visually impressive, with a design of its own: a dark gallery that lets the work stand out.
- Display type that is well proportioned, not overly wide.
- Responsive. It has to look good on a phone as well as a desktop.

## Content and pages

- Award categories with deadlines, a gallery of entries and past winners.
- Working forms to enter work and to nominate someone, with spam protection.
- Submissions must not be published automatically.

## Done means

- `raytha check` passes with no broken routes.
- Screenshots of the result on desktop and mobile.
- A kit in this repository that rebuilds the site on a fresh install.
