# Brief: Meridian Hub, a company intranet / employee portal

The brief as the agent got it.

> Build a company intranet / employee portal on a fresh Raytha instance with the `raytha` CLI. No clicking around the admin.

## Look and feel

- Visually impressive, with a design of its own. No wide display fonts.
- Responsive. It has to look good on a phone as well as a desktop.

## Content and pages

- A public landing / sign-in page for employees.
- Announcements, policies and handbook, department and people directory, events, resources and documents, and IT help articles.
- Most of the portal is employees-only (Raytha login + user groups).
- No payment or registration forms.

## Done means

- `raytha check` passes with no broken routes.
- Screenshots of the result on desktop and mobile, and a walkthrough video.
- A kit in this repository that rebuilds the site on a fresh install.
