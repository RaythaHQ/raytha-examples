# Brief: Corrowen & Ivesmark LLP — a law firm website (fictional)
Persona: Corrowen & Ivesmark LLP (fictional), a seven-lawyer litigation and business law firm with two offices in the invented river city of Wendlow. The same build fits a firm's own marketing team, or an agency or freelancer building sites for law firm clients.
Design: "redline" — the site reads like a marked-up legal draft. Bone paper, ink black, a vermilion red-pencil accent and a tracked-changes blue. A fixed left rail works as a numbered table of contents (§ 1 Practice, § 2 Attorneys...). The home hero is a clause with tracked changes (deletions struck, insertions underlined) and margin comments pulled from each attorney's margin_note field. Practice areas appear as a table of contents with dotted leaders and matter counts; representative matters appear as a court docket with disposition stamps; attorneys appear as signature blocks; insights appear as memos (TO / FROM / RE / DATE). Source Serif 4 for text and headings, IBM Plex Sans for interface text, IBM Plex Mono for section numbers and docket entries. No wide display fonts.
IA: home; /practice (areas, with what the firm refers out and how it bills), /attorneys, /matters (docket), /insights, /offices, /library (public, client and in-house counsel documents), /client-room (members-only), /firm, /notices, /search.
Members: user groups `clients` and `counsel`; gating is server-side in Liquid with CurrentUser.UserGroups. Gated bodies are never sent to the browser.
No contact, intake, payment or registration forms. Everything is labeled fictional; phone numbers use 555. Attorney advertising and no-attorney-client-relationship notices on every page.
Buyer target: self-hosted CMS for law firms / open-source .NET CMS for a law firm website.
Done: raytha check 0 failed, screenshots, walkthrough, rebuildable kit.
