# Brief: Noor Halvorsen for County Commission — a political campaign website (fictional)
Persona: Noor Halvorsen (fictional), a former county extension agent running for the nonpartisan District 2 seat on the Wrenfield County Commission (fictional). The election is Tuesday, April 6, 2027. District 2 has eight precincts along the Wren River (Ashby Ford, Quillon Mills, Marrow Hill, Sexton Ridge, Tannery Flats, Hollin Cross, Pell Orchard, Fallowmere). The campaign has a field organizer, a campaign manager, precinct captains and volunteers. The site is built for the campaign by a consultant, agency or freelancer, or by the campaign itself. No party is named or implied.
Design: "yard sign and sample ballot" — a deep green and marigold yard sign on wire stakes, a ballot with ovals, cream paper and dark ink. Headings in Source Serif 4, body text in Public Sans, dates and labels in IBM Plex Mono. No wide display fonts. Colors that stay clear of party colors. Nothing like a realtor listing window, a park kiosk or a charity station board.
IA: home (yard sign, Election Day countdown, road-to-Election-Day rail, plan as a sample ballot, district map, upcoming events, questionnaire answers, endorsements); /plan and plan item pages (what, cost, measure; previous/next ballot item); /district and precinct pages (polling place, turnout, events in the precinct); /vote (precinct lookup with a GET form, deadlines); /trail and event pages; /answers (filter by topic) and answer pages; /endorsements; /road (all dates with countdowns); /notes; /about; /help; /volunteers (volunteers only); /staff (staff only); /shifts (volunteers only); /search.
Server-side logic: days to each milestone and the "today" position on the road rail; events split into today / coming up / already happened; precinct highlighting on the map from a query parameter; turf map shading from doors knocked against the goal; staff table flags for precincts behind.
Members: user groups `volunteers` and `staff`; gating is server-side in Liquid with CurrentUser.UserGroups. Gated content is never sent to the browser.
No donation, payment, registration or sign-up forms. Everything is labeled fictional; phone numbers use 555.
Buyer target: self-hosted CMS for political campaigns / open-source .NET CMS for candidate websites; campaigns and the consultants, agencies and freelancers who build for them.
Done: raytha check 0 failed, screenshots, walkthrough, rebuildable kit.
